Privacy

Plain-language summary of what this demo collects. Last updated November 2026.

What we never collect from players:
  • No student accounts, sign-ups, or passwords.
  • No real names, birthdays, addresses, or photos from players.
  • No advertising and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No tracking across other websites.

Game progress stays on your device

Heroes, gear, and progress are saved in your own browser on your own computer. They are not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, your progress is erased unless you made a 🔑 Save Code.

What is sent, and why

Students are asked not to type their name, email, or school into feedback. The Full Version form tells students to ask a teacher or parent instead of filling it in.

Where it's stored and who can see it

Feedback, requests, and stats are stored in Google Firebase. Only the game's creator can read them; the database is locked so no one else can. Full Version requests are also emailed to schoolhiddentreasuregame@gmail.com through FormSubmit, an email-forwarding service, so the game maker can reply quickly. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, those services can see basic technical information such as an IP address when your browser connects. That information is not saved with any game data.

School codes and teacher sign-in (full version)

The full version opens only with a school code. When a student or teacher types a school code, we save which school's code was used and a random ID for that device, so the game knows the device is allowed in. No student names or accounts are involved.

Teachers can sign in with Google to run online classes. For them we store their Google email and account ID so they can manage their own classes. Each teacher can see only their own students' progress; students appear by the hero name they choose.

Removing your information

To have something you sent removed (for example, a Full Version request), email schoolhiddentreasuregame@gmail.com, send a message with the 💬 Feedback button that says what to remove, or reply to any email you received from us. Anonymous stats have no name attached, so they can't be traced back to a person.

Schools

The demo does not need student accounts, and students can play without giving any personal information. Schools that buy the full version get a school code for their devices. Questions about privacy or a district agreement? Email schoolhiddentreasuregame@gmail.com.
