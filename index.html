Questions

What devices does it run on?

Chromebooks and Windows or Mac computers with a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari). Nothing to install.

Do students need accounts or email addresses?

No. Students just type a hero name. Progress saves on the device, and a Save Code moves it to another computer.

Does it need the internet?

Only to load the page the first time. After that, the demo works offline. You can also install it like an app (look for 📲 Install, or the install icon in Chrome's address bar).

What information does it collect?

No student accounts or names. Progress stays on the device. The demo sends anonymous play stats and any feedback you choose to send. See the privacy page.

What standards does it cover?

Grades 5–8 Science, Social Studies, ELA, and Math, tagged to Oklahoma Academic Standards.
