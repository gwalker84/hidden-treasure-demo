Hidden Treasure Quest

Teacher Quick-Start Guide

Up and running in about ten minutes.
← Back to the game page
Play the demo

1Open the game

  • Website: open the game link in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  • Downloaded file: double-click HiddenTreasureQuest.html. It opens in the browser and works offline.
  • Students play in a normal browser window, not Incognito, so their progress is saved.

2Set your name and password

Your name appears in the story, and the password protects the Teacher Hub.

Downloaded version: open the file in Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac). Near the top, change these two lines and save:
window.HTQ_TEACHER = "Mrs. Smith";
window.HTQ_PASSWORD = "your-password";

You can also change the name in 🍎 Teacher Hub → 🎛️ Options. The default password is teacher. Change it before students play.

3Choose what students practice

Click 🍎 Teacher Hub on the title screen and type your password. Then:

🎛️ Options
Pick the subject (Science, Social Studies, ELA, or Math), or let each student choose. Remember to click 💾 Save Settings.
📋 Assign & Test
Create assignments and tests for your students.
📏 Standards
Focus on specific Oklahoma Academic Standards.
❓ Questions
Set the question grade level and add your own custom questions.
📖 Vocab
Add vocabulary lists that show up as word battles.
🤝 Student Supports
Accommodations for individual students (see step 7).

4Students make heroes

  • Click ⚔️ New Hero, type a hero name, pick a class, and start.
  • Have students use a nickname or first name only. No email or account is needed.
  • 2–3 students can share one computer with 👥 Party Play.

5Saving and switching computers

  • Progress saves automatically on that computer and browser.
  • To move to another computer, students tap 🔑 Save Code at camp, copy the code, and keep it (Google Classroom, a doc, or email). On the new computer, they choose 🔑 Load Save Code on the title screen.
Tip: Have students make a new Save Code at the end of each class. Clearing browser data erases saves, and a code brings them back.

6Collect scores

  1. At the end of class, students tap 📤 Today's Score at camp and copy their Score Code.
  2. They paste it where you collect work (Google Classroom, a form, or a chat).
  3. In the Teacher Hub, paste the codes into 📋 Progress Report to see accuracy by standard, download a gradebook CSV, or print parent letters.

7Supports and rewards

  • 🤝 Student Supports: turn on extra time, read-aloud, fewer answer choices, and more for individual students.
  • 👓 Reading & Display (title screen): bigger text, an easier-to-read font, high contrast, and less motion.
  • 🎁 Rewards & Events: create reward codes students redeem at camp.
  • 🔊 Read aloud: students tap 🔊 or press R in battle.

8Classroom tips

  • Use ⏰ Class Timer at camp so students get a warning before time is up.
  • Wrong answers that come too fast cost a little gold, so encourage students to read every question.
  • Missed questions come back later as 👻 Mistake Monsters. That's the review built into the game.
